# Voice AI Lead Qualifier

An AI-powered voice agent that qualifies inbound leads, handles objections, and books appointments automatically - 24/7.

## The Problem

You're losing leads every night. Your ads are running. Leads are coming in. But by the time your sales team calls back, 60% have already bought from your competitor.

**The average B2B lead response time is 47 hours.**
**But 78% of buyers go with the first vendor who responds.**

## The Solution

This Voice AI Lead Qualifier answers calls instantly, asks the right questions, handles objections, and books qualified leads directly into your calendar.

## What It Does

1. **Answers instantly** - 24/7, never misses a call
2. **Qualifies leads** - Asks about budget, timeline, pain points
3. **Handles objections** - "Too expensive", "Need to think", etc.
4. **Books appointments** - Directly into your Google Calendar
5. **Notifies your team** - Slack alert for hot leads
6. **Sends confirmation** - SMS to the lead

## Lead Qualification Questions

1. What industry is your business in?
2. What's your biggest challenge right now?
3. What's your timeline to solve this?
4. What's your budget range?
5. Are you the decision-maker?

## Tech Stack

VAPI/Retell AI + OpenAI GPT-4 + Google Calendar + HubSpot
